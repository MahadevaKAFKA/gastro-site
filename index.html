GASTRO

GASTRO is a personal desktop assistant for Windows, with a companion Android app. Among other things, it edits short vertical videos and, when its owner asks it to, uploads them to the owner's own TikTok accounts through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

GASTRO is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, with the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

How it uses TikTok:

  • The owner signs in to their TikTok account with TikTok's own Login Kit and grants access.
  • GASTRO uploads videos the owner has made, either as drafts in the TikTok inbox or as posts, as the owner chooses.
  • GASTRO reads the basic profile (display name) to show which account is connected, and the public numbers of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares) to report how they are doing.
