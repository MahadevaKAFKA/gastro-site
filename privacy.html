Effective 17 November 2026.

GASTRO is a personal desktop application used by its owner with the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. This policy explains what it does with information from those services.

What GASTRO accesses

  • Basic profile (TikTok scope user.info.basic): the account's open ID, display name and avatar, to show which account is connected.
  • Video upload and posting (video.upload, video.publish): to send videos the owner made to the owner's own account, as drafts or posts.
  • The owner's own videos (video.list): public counts such as views, likes, comments and shares, to report how the owner's posts are doing.

Where it is kept

Everything stays on the owner's own computer: the access tokens TikTok issues, the list of posts and their numbers. Nothing is sent to any server run by GASTRO (there is none), and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

How long

Data is kept until the owner removes the account from GASTRO or deletes GASTRO's data folder. Access can also be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (in its settings, under the apps that have access to the account).

Other people

GASTRO is not used to collect information about other people. It does not access other users' accounts, messages or personal data.

Children

GASTRO is not intended for children and is not offered to the public.

Changes and contact
